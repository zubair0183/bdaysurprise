A Note For Shree

Happy birthday, Shree.

You knew when we met, it was just an accident. Who would have thought that it would lead to this? At first, you seemed to be busy, but no one can stop God’s decision. We were made to be friends, so we are.

I don’t know what you think about me, or how you see our friendship, but you will always be my bestest friend in my life. I’m genuinely glad that somehow, that random meeting happened and brought you into my life.

There have been so many small moments, conversations, laughs, and memories that I’ll always remember. Maybe at that time we never thought they would become this important, but somehow they did.

I just hope that no matter where life takes us, we always stay the same friends who can talk, laugh, annoy each other, and still be there when it actually matters.

You are someone I’m really grateful to have met, and I hope this friendship stays for a very, very long time.

Happy birthday once again, Shree.

Here’s to another year of your life, and hopefully many more memories for us to make together.

Always, Your Best Friend
